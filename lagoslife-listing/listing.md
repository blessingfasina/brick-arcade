## Short description (115 of 120 characters)
Nine retro brick handheld classics, from Snake to Brick Stack, plus a daily challenge that ranks you on Lagos Life.

Alternative (114 characters):
Snake, Brick Breaker, Pong and six more brick handheld classics. Beat the daily challenge on the Lagos Life board.

## Long description
Remember the little grey brick game handheld? Retro Classic Games brings nine of its classics to Lagos Life, rebuilt for your phone with the same chunky pixels, blips and bloops.

THE GAMES
- Snake: eat, grow and don't bite your own tail.
- Brick Breaker: smash every brick and clear the level.
- Brick Racer: two lanes, endless traffic. Hold boost if you dare.
- Brick Stack: drop the blocks, clear lines, don't top out.
- Pong: you against the computer, first to five.
- Tanks: hold the line against wave after wave of tanks.
- Road Crossing: hop through the traffic to the top.
- Invaders: one ship against the marching formation.
- Flappy: tap to flap. One life. No mercy.

DAILY CHALLENGE
Every day one game is the daily challenge, with the same layout, level and speed for every player. Daily challenge scores go on the Lagos Life leaderboard, so it's a fair fight. The other games are for practice and your own personal best.

PLAY YOUR WAY
- Big on-screen buttons made for playing one-handed in portrait.
- Before you start, pick your starting level and speed, from 1 to 10. Faster speeds score more.
- Three screen styles free: 3D Bricks, Neon Arcade and Classic LCD.
- Eight console colours, big buttons and a high-contrast screen option.

OPTIONAL EXTRAS
Everything above is free. If you want to support the game, you can unlock:
- Premium Console Colours: gold, chrome and jade.
- Amber CRT Screen: a glowing amber retro monitor look.
- Mascot Outfits: a crown, cap or shades for your little pixel buddy.
- Daily Archive: replay any of the last 30 daily challenges as practice.
- Everything Bundle: all four together.

The extras only change how the game looks, or let you practise old dailies. They never affect gameplay or scores, and nothing can be bought to climb the leaderboard. No ads.
