How to play Brick Stack

  1. Move the falling piece with the left and right arrows or the yellow buttons.
  2. Rotate with Up, Space or the blue button. Hold Down to drop faster and earn a point per row.
  3. Fill a complete row to clear it. Clearing several rows at once scores much more.
  4. Every ten lines the level goes up and pieces fall faster. The game ends when the stack reaches the top.

About this game

The block-stacking puzzle that made the brick handheld famous. Seven pieces, a ten-wide well, and a next-piece preview in the side panel so you can plan ahead. Pieces are dealt in shuffled sets of seven, so you will never wait forever for a long bar. Scoring rewards clearing four lines at once, so keep one column open and be brave.
