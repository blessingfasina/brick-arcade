How to play Flappy

  1. Press Space, Up or the blue button to flap. Gravity does the rest.
  2. Fly through the gap in each pipe. Every pipe you pass scores points.
  3. Every five pipes the level goes up, the pipes come faster and the gaps get tighter.
  4. One touch of a pipe, the ceiling or the floor and it is over. Press START to go again.

About this game

Flappy is the one that ruins lunch breaks. On the brick screen the bird is two cells, the pipes are two columns wide, and the gap shrinks from six rows to four as you level up. The trick is small, early taps rather than big rescues: keep the bird in the middle of the screen and only flap when it starts to dip.
