SCORE 000000

  • The original. Flat grey cells, exactly like the little handheld.

HOW SCREEN STYLES WORK

  • One choice for every game. The style you pick here, or under SETTING on any game, applies to all nine games on this device.
  • Only the look and sound change. Gameplay, levels, daily challenges and the world leaderboard are identical in every style.
  • The screen leans with your phone in 3D Bricks and Neon Arcade. On iPhone, turn on "Tilt with phone" under SETTING to allow it.
  • Sound follows the style. Volume and mute are under SETTING and the SOUND button on every console. An iPhone on silent mutes website sounds.
