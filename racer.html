How to play Brick Racer

  1. Tap left or right, A or D, or the yellow buttons to switch between the two lanes.
  2. Cars come down the road toward you. Every car you pass scores points.
  3. Hold Space or the blue button to boost. You go much faster and score double for every car passed.
  4. Every ten cars the level rises and the road speeds up. You have three lives.

About this game

Brick Racer is the racing game from the 9999-in-1 style handhelds: a road made of dashes, a car made of seven cells and traffic that never ends. The trick is rhythm. Watch the top of the screen, not your own car, and switch lanes early. Boost is where the big scores are, but the road moves more than twice as fast, so use it in short bursts when the lane ahead is clear.
