How to play Pong

  1. Move your paddle at the bottom with the left and right arrows or the yellow buttons.
  2. Press Space, Up or the blue button to serve.
  3. Score a point when the computer misses. First to five wins the round and the game speeds up.
  4. If the computer reaches five first, the game is over. Your total points are your score.

About this game

Pong is the oldest video game most people can name, and it fits the brick handheld perfectly. The computer paddle reacts late and sometimes hesitates for a whole rally, so early rounds are winnable. From about level five it gets sharp. Hit the ball with the edge of your paddle to send it off at an angle and make the computer chase it.
